AUGUSTĪNĪ SERMŌ XIII






SERMŌ <HABITŪS|HABITUS> AD MĒNSAM SĀNCTĪ CYPRIĀNĪ VĪ [KAL+] [IUN+] DĒ PSALMŌ UBI DĪCIT [ERUDIMINI OMNĒS QUĪ <JŪDICĀTĪS|JŪDICĀTIS> [TERRAM]] [erudimini omnēs quī <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> [terram].] terram jūdicāre <est|ēst> corpus domāre. audiāmus apostolum jūdicantem terram: [non sīc [pugno]], inquit, [tamquam āerem caedēns, sed castīgō corpus meum et in servitūtem redigō, nē forte aliīs praedicāns ipse reprobus [efficiar].] audī ergō <terra|terrā> jūdicantem terram, et jūdicā terram nē sīs <terra|terrā>. sī enim terram <jūdicāverīs|jūdicāveris>, caelum <eris|erīs>, et in tē factam glōriam dominī [enarrābis]. [caeli] enim [enarrant glōriam [dei].] sī autem terram nōn <jūdicāverīs|jūdicāveris>, <terra|terrā> <eris|erīs>. sī autem <terra|terrā> <fueris|fuerīs>, ad eum [pertinēbis] cui dictum <est|ēst>: [terram [manducabis].] audiant ergō jūdicēs terrae. corpus castīgent, libīdinēs frēnent, ament sapientiam, vincant concupiscentiam. et ut <hoc|hōc> faciant, ērudiantur. haec <est|ēst> autem <summa|summā> ērudītiōnis: [seruite dominō in timōre et <exultāte|exultātē> <eī|ei> cum [tremore]], exultāre <eī|ei>, nōn sibi: <eī|ei> ā quō <est|ēst> quod <es|ēs>, et quod homō <es|ēs>, et quod jūstus <es|ēs>, sī tamen jam jūstus <es|ēs>. sī autem <putāveris|putāverīs> quod ab illō quidem homō <es|ēs>, ā tē autem jūstus <es|ēs>, nōn servīs dominō in timōre, nec exultās <eī|ei> in tremōre, sed tibi in praesūmptiōne. et quid tibi fīet, <nisi|nīsī> quod sequitur? [ne quandō īrāscātur dominus et [pereatis]], inquit, [de <via|viā> [iusta].] nōn enim ait: nē quandō īrāscātur dominus, et nōn intrētis in viam jūstam, sed: [pereatis dē <viā|via> [iusta].] jam enim <vīderīs|vīderis|vidēris> tibi jūstus, nōn rapiendō aliēnum, nōn [adulterando], nōn homicīdium faciendō, nōn falsum testimōnium adversus proximum dīcendō, patrem et mātrem honōrandō, ūnum deum colendō, īdōlīs et daemoniīs nōn serviendō. dē hāc <viā|via> perībis, sī tibi <ista|istā> <praesūmpseris|praesūmpserīs>, sī tibi <ista|istā> ā tē ipsō <esse|ēsse> <putāverīs|putāveris>. īnfidēlēs enim nōn intrant in viam jūstam, superbī pereunt dē <viā|via> <jūstā|jūsta>. quid enim ait? [erudimini quī <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> [terram].] et nē forte <vīrēs|virēs> et potentiam, <quā|qua> <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> terram, vōbīs tribuātis, ā vōbīs ipsīs eam vōbīs <esse|ēsse> crēdātis, nōlīte sīc: [seruite dominō in timōre; [exultate]], nōn vōbīs in praesūmptiōne, sed [ei in timōre, nē quandō īrāscātur dominus, et pereātis dē <viā|via> <jūstā|jūsta>, cum exārserit <cito|citō> <īra|īrā> [eius].] quid ergō faciendum <est|ēst> nē pereāmus dē <viā|via> <jūstā|jūsta>? [beati omnēs quī cōnfīdunt in [eum].] sī beātī quī cōnfīdunt in eum, miserī quī cōnfīdunt in sē. [maledictus] enim [omnis homō quī spem suam pōnit in [homine]], ergō nec in tē, quia et tū homō <es|ēs>. sī enim spem tuam <posueris|posuerīs> in aliō homine, <inōrdinātē|inōrdināte> <humilis|humilīs> <eris|erīs>. sī autem spem tuam <posueris|posuerīs> in tē, <perīculōsē|perīculōse> superbīs. quid autem [interest]? utrumque perniciōsum, nihil hōrum ēligendum. <inōrdinātē|inōrdināte> <humilis|humilīs> nōn <levātur|lēvātur>, <perīculōsē|perīculōse> superbus praecipitātur. dēnique ut nōverit sānctitās <vestra|vestrā>, propter istum sēnsum [redarguendum] et cōnsūmendum, quod in sē quisque cōnfīdit, <dicta|dictā> <esse|ēsse> <ista|istā> verba: [seruite dominō in timōre et <exultāte|exultātē> <eī|ei> cum [tremore]], apostolum <audīte|audītē> haec <ipsa|ipsā> verba dīcentem, et sententiam cūr <dicta|dictā> sit explānantem. ecce apostolī verba sunt: [cum timōre et tremōre vestram ipsōrum salūtem [operamini].] quārē ergō cum timōre et tremōre eam salūtem operor, cum sit in potestāte <meā|mea> operārī salūtem meam? vīs audīre quārē cum timōre et tremōre? [deus <est|ēst> enim quī operātur in [uobis].] ideō cum timōre et tremōre. quia quod impetrat <humilis|humilīs>, āmittit superbus. sī ergō deus <est|ēst> quī operātur in nōbīs, quārē dictum <est|ēst>: [uestram ipsōrum salūtem [operamini]]? quia sīc in nōbīs operātur, ut et nōs operēmur. [adiutor meus [esto].] dēsignat et sē [operātōrem], quī invocat adjūtōrem. sed voluntās inquit <mea|meā> <bona|bonā> <est|ēst>. fateor, <tua|tuā>. sed et <ipsa|ipsā> ā quō <data|datā>, ā quō <excitāta|excitātā>? nōlī mē ipsum audīre, apostolum interrogā: [deus <est|ēst> [enim]], inquit, [qui operātur in vōbīs et [uelle]] - operātur in vōbīs et velle - [et operārī prō <bonā|bona> [uoluntate].] quid <est|ēst> ergō quod tibi [arrogabas]? quid <est|ēst> quod superbus ībās et [perībās]? redī ad cor tuum, <invēnī|invenī> tē <malum|mālum>, et ut sīs bonus invocā bonum. nōn enim in tē <placet|plācet> deō, <nisi|nīsī> quod habēs ex deō; quod autem habēs ex tē, displicet deō. sī <bona|bonā> <tua|tuā> cōgitās, [quid habēs quod nōn accēpistī? sī autem accēpistī, quid <glōriāris|glōriārīs> quasi nōn [acceperis]]? <sōlus|solus|solūs> <est|ēst> ille quī nōn nōvit <nisi|nīsī> dare. nōn habet datōrem, quī nōn habet meliōrem. quō tū sī īnferior <es|ēs>, immō quia īnferior <es|ēs>, grātulāre quod ad ejus imāginem <factus|factūs> <es|ēs>, ut in illō [inueniaris] quī in tē peristī. nōn enim potuistī in tē <nisi|nīsī> <perdere|perdēre> tē; nescīs invenīre tē, <nisi|nīsī> ille quī fēcit tē quaerat tē. alloquāmur tamen et eōs quī secundum istam vīsibilem et populārem intellegentiam jūdicant terram. jūdicant enim terram <rēgēs|regēs>, ducēs, prīncipēs, jūdicēs. [unusquisque] prō mūnere quod accēpit in <terrā|terra> jūdicat terram. quid <est|ēst> autem quod dīcitur jūdicat terram, <nisi|nīsī> jūdicat hominēs quī sunt in <terrā|terra>? nam sī terram propriē nōn accipiās <nisi|nīsī> quam calcās, agricolīs ergō dictum <est|ēst>: [qui <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> [terram].] porrō autem sī rēgēs jūdicant terram, et quīcumque sub rēgibus potestātem accipiunt ā rēgibus, ērudiantur et ipsī, quia <terra|terrā> jūdicat terram, et timēre dēbet eum quī <est|ēst> in caelō <terra|terrā> jūdicāns terram. parem quippe suum jūdicat, homō hominem, <mortālis|mortālīs> mortālem, peccātor peccātōrem. sī enim prōcēdat in <medium|mēdium> <dominicā|dominica> <illā|illa> <sententia|sententiā>: [qui <sine|sīne> peccātō <est|ēst> prior in illam lapidem [mittat]], nōnne <omnis|omnīs> jūdicāns terram terrae mōtum patitur? ēvangelicum capitulum <recolāmus|recōlāmus>. <Pharisaeī|Pharīsaeī> tentantēs dominum, addūxērunt ante eum mulierem in adulteriō dēprehēnsam, dē quō peccātō <poena|poenā> fuerat <lēge|lege> <dēfīnītā|dēfīnīta>, <lēge|lege> scīlicet <datā|data> per [Moysēn] famulum deī. hāc ergō complexiōne <dolōsa|dolōsā> et <fraudulenta|fraudulentā> ad dominum <Pharisaeī|Pharīsaeī> accessērunt, ut sī jubēret [lapidari] mulierem [diffamatam], perderet mānsuētūdinem. sī autem quod lēx jusserat prohibēret, peccāsse tenērētur in lēgem. sīcut autem interrogantēs dē tribūtō Caesarī reddendō ōre suō cēpit, vicissim interrogāns cujus <esset|ēsset> <prōlātus|prōlātūs> nummus, cujus habēret imāginem et [superscrīptiōnem]. respondentēs ipsī quī interrogāvērunt, in nummō <esse|ēsse> imāginem Caesaris, respondit eīs secundum <os|ōs> ipsōrum: [reddite Caesarī quae Caesaris sunt, et deō quae deī [sunt]], ut admonēret sīc reddendam deō in homine imāginem deī, quemadmodum in nummō imāgō <sua|suā> redditur Caesarī. ita et in <istā|ista> <adulterā|adultera> [interrogātōrēs] interrogāvit, et ideō jūdicēs jūdicāvit. nōn prohibeō inquit [lapidari] quam [lapidari] lēx jussit, sed quaerō ā quibus. nōn enim resistō, sed <lēgis|legis> ministrum requīrō. dēnique <audīte|audītē>: lapidāre vultis secundum lēgem? [qui <sine|sīne> peccātō <est|ēst>, prior in illam lapidem [mittat].] quandō autem haec audīvit, [digito scrībēbat in [terra]], ut ērudīret terram. quandō autem <hoc|hōc> dīxit <Pharisaeīs|Pharīsaeīs>, <levāvit|lēvāvit> oculōs suōs, et īnspexit terram et fēcit eam <tremere|tremēre>. proinde cum <hoc|hōc> dīxisset, iterum coepit <scrībere|scrībēre> in <terrā|terra>. at illī compūnctī et tremefactī, ūnus post alterum discessērunt. ō terrae mōtum, ubi sīc <terra|terrā> <mōta|mōtā> <est|ēst>, ut etiam mūtāret locum! illīs ergō discēdentibus, remānsit peccātrīx et salvātor. remānsit <aegrōta|aegrōtā> et medicus. remānsit <misera|miserā> et <misericordia|misericordiā>. et aspiciēns mulierem dīxit: [nemo tē [condemnauit]]? et <illa|illā>: [nemo, [domine].] sed tamen adhūc <sollicita|sollicitā> erat. peccātōrēs enim damnāre nōn ausī sunt, nōn ausī sunt lapidāre peccātrīcem quī sē ipsōs intuentēs similēs invēnērunt. sed adhūc mulier erat in magnō perīculō, quia ille <eī|ei> jūdex remānserat quī erat sine peccātō. [nemo], inquit, [te [damnauit]]? at <illa|illā>: [nemo [domine]]: sī nec tū, <sēcūra|sēcūrā> sum. cui sollicitūdinī <tacitā|tacita> dominus vōce respondit: [nec ego tē [damnabo].] [nec [ego]], quamvīs sim <sine|sīne> peccātō, [nec ego tē [damnabo].] illōs ā <vindictā|vindicta> repressit <cōnscientiā|cōnscientia>, mē ad subveniendum inclīnat <misericordia|misericordiā>. haec attendite, et [erudimini omnēs quī <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> [terram].] omnēs scīlicet, quia etiam sīc intellegendum <est|ēst> dē quibus apostolus dīcit: [omnis <anima|animā> potestātibus sublīmiōribus <subdita|subditā> sit. nōn <est|ēst> enim potestās, <nisi|nīsī> ā deō. quae autem ā deō sunt, <ōrdināta|ōrdinātā> sunt. quī resistit potestātī, deī ōrdinātiōnī resistit. prīncipēs enim nōn sunt timōrī bonī <operis|operīs>, sed <mālī|malī>. vīs autem nōn timēre potestātem? bonum fac, et habēbis laudem ex [illa].] et sī nōn ab <illā|illa>, tamen ex <illā|illa>. aut enim <jūstē|jūste> agis, et <jūsta|jūstā> potestās laudābit tē; aut <jūstē|jūste> agentem, etiam sī potestās <injūsta|injūstā> damnet tē, deus jūstus corōnābit tē. ac per <hoc|hōc> tū jūstitiam tenē, tū bene <vīve|vīvē>; et sī damnet sīve absolvat, habēbis laudem ex <illā|illa>. beātus ille, cujus <hīc|hic> sanguis <fūsus|fūsūs> <est|ēst>, nōnne ex <ipsā|ipsa> potestāte, antequam et ā <quā|qua> <vīsus|vīsūs> <est|ēst> jūdicārī, laudem <invēnit|invenit>? cōnfessiōnem [promsit], in <fide|fidē|fīde|fīdē> permānsit, mortem nōn timuit, sanguinem fūdit, diabolum vīcit. ut ergō nōn <sītis|sitis|sitīs> inīquae potestātis, quīcumque hominēs vultis habēre in hominēs potestātem, ērudīminī nē <perversē|perverse> jūdicētis, et ante in <animā|anima> pereātis quam quemquam in carne [perdātis]. jūdex <esse|ēsse> vīs, nōn <potes|pōtēs> meritīs, vel <pecūniā|pecūnia>. nōndum reprehendō. fortasse enim prōdesse <cupis|cūpīs> rēbus hūmānīs, et emis ut prōsīs. ut serviās jūstitiae, nōn <parcis|parcīs> pecūniae. prius propter tē <estō|ēsto> jūdex in tē. prius jūdicā dē tē, ut dē penetrālī cōnscientiae sēcūrus prōcēdās ad alterum. in tē ipsum redī, tē attende, tē discute, tē audī. ibi tē volō probāre integrum jūdicem, ubi nōn quaeris testem. <prōcēdere|prōcēdēre> vīs cum potestāte ut alter tibi <dīcat|dicat> dē alterō quod tū nescīs. prius intus jūdicā. nihil tibi dē tē dīxit <cōnscientia|cōnscientiā> <tua|tuā>? sī <nōlīs|nolīs> negāre, utique dīxit. nōlō audīre quid dīxerit, tū jūdicā quī audīstī. dīxit tibi dē tē quid <fēceris|fēcerīs>, quid <accēperīs|accēperis>, quid <peccāverīs|peccāveris>. vellem scīre quālem sententiam prōtulistī. sī bene audīstī, sī <rēctē|rēcte> audīstī, sī in audiendō tē jūstus fuistī, sī tuae <mentis|mentīs> tribūnal ascendistī, sī tē ipsum ante tē ipsum in eculeum <cordis|cordīs> [suspēndistī], sī gravēs tortōrēs adhibuistī timōris, bene audīstī sī sīc audīstī, et procul dubiō paenitendō peccātum pūnīstī. ecce et discussistī, et audīstī, et pūnīstī. et tamen tibi pepercistī. sīc audī et proximum tuum, sī [erudiāris] sīcut admonuit psalmus: [erudimini quī <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> [terram].] sī sīc audīs proximum tuum quōmodo audīs et tē, <peccāta|peccātā> <persequeris|persequēris>, nōn peccantem. et sī forte quisquam dūrus <est|ēst> ad <corrigenda|corrigendā> <peccāta|peccātā> āversus ā timōre deī, <hoc|hōc> ipsum in eō <persequeris|persequēris>, <hoc|hōc> ipsum <corrigere|corrigēre> cōnāberis, <hoc|hōc> <perdere|perdēre> et tollere labōrābis, ut servētur homō peccātō damnātō. duo enim <nōmina|nōminā> sunt, homō et peccātor. hominem deus fēcit, peccātōrem sē ipse homō fēcit. pereat quod fēcit homō, <līberētur|libērētur> quod fēcit deus. nōlī ergō usque ad mortem, nē cum <persequeris|persequēris> peccātum, perdās hominem. nōlī usque ad mortem, ut sī quem paeniteat, homō nōn necētur ut sit quem paeniteat; homō nōn necētur ut sit quī ēmendētur. hanc in corde retinēns homō in hominēs dīlēctiōnem, <estō|ēsto> jūdex terrae. et amā terrēre, sed dīlige. sī superbīs, superbī in <peccāta|peccātā>, nōn in hominem. in illud saevī quod tibi displicet et in tē, nōn in eum quī <factus|factūs> <est|ēst> sīcut tū. dē <ūnā|ūna> <officīnā|officīna> existis, ūnum artificem habuistis, ūnus līmus <est|ēst> <vestra|vestrā> māteriēs. quid perdis nōn <amandō|āmandō> quem jūdicās? quoniam jūstitiam perdis, nōn <amandō|āmandō> quem jūdicās. sed adhibeantur poenae. nōn recūsō, nōn interdīcō, sed animō <amantis|amantīs>, animō <dīligentis|dīligentīs>, animō [corrigentis]. nōn enim fīlium tuum nōn [erūdis]. et prius agis, ut sī fierī potest pudōre et līberālitāte ērudiātur, ērubēscat patrem <offendere|offendēre>, nōn tamquam sevērum jūdicem timeat. gaudēs ad tālem fīlium. sed sī fuerit forte <ista|istā> contemnēns, adhibēs et <verbera|verberā>, [incutis] poenam, ingeris dolōrem, sed quaeris salūtem. multī amōre, multī timōre corrēctī sunt, sed per tremōrem timōris ad amōrem pervēnērunt. [erudimini quī <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> [terram].] dīligite et <jūdicāte|jūdicātē>. nōn enim sīc quaeritur <innocentia|innocentiā>, ut pereat <disciplīna|disciplīnā>. scrīptum <est|ēst>: [disciplinam quī abicit īnfēlīx [est].] bene potest addī ad istam sententiam: sīcut disciplīnam quī abicit īnfēlīx <est|ēst>, sīc disciplīnam quī negat <crūdēlis|crūdēlīs> <est|ēst>. <ausus|ausūs> sum aliquid <dīcere|dicēre|dīcēre>, frātrēs meī, quod vōbīs aliquantō plēnius <expōnere|expōnēre> ipsīus reī obscūritāte compellor. repetō quod dīxī: disciplīnam quī abicit īnfēlīx <est|ēst>. <hoc|hōc> manifēstum <est|ēst>. disciplīnam quī nōn dat <crūdēlis|crūdēlīs> <est|ēst>. teneō prōrsus, teneō et ostendō feriendō pium, parcendō crūdēlem. exemplum ante oculōs pōnō. ubi inveniō feriendō pium? nōn eō ad aliōs, ad patrem et fīlium eō. pater et quandō ferit, amat. et nōn vult puer caedī. contemnit voluntātem, cōnsulit ad ūtilitātem. quārē? quia pater <est|ēst>, quia hērēditātem <parat|pārat>, quia successōrem nūtrit. ecce feriendō pius <est|ēst> pater, feriendō misericors <est|ēst>. dā mihi hominem parcendō crūdēlem. nōn recēdō ā <persōnīs|personīs>, eōs ipsōs ante oculōs pōnō. sed puer imperītus et [indisciplīnātus] sī sīc vīvat ut pereat, et pater dissimulet, pater parcat, pater perditum fīlium disciplīnae asperitāte <offendere|offendēre> timeat, nōnne parcendō <crūdēlis|crūdēlīs> <est|ēst>? [erudimini] ergō [qui <jūdicātis|jūdicātīs> [terram]], et bene jūdicandō, nōn ā <terrā|terra> praemium sed ab illō <expectāte|expectātē> quī fēcit caelum et terram.
